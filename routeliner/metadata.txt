about=Linear referencing for QGIS with full support of CIS chainage practice: station formats (ПК 15+35, км+м, railway km/picket), picket ledgers with station equations (forward and backward), several chainage systems per route. Routes are assembled from shuffled multipart pieces and true arcs. Every run reports unplaced records with the reason. Includes a demo generator with analytically computed reference answers. Pure PyQGIS + NumPy, no external dependencies.
